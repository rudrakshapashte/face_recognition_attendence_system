Automatic Attendance

Register a student's face once, then use the laptop webcam to recognize the student and mark attendance.

Register Student

How it works

  1. Register student name and roll number.
  2. Capture 30 face samples using webcam.
  3. Train the LBPH face recognition model.
  4. Start attendance and look at the camera.
  5. Attendance is saved once per student per day.
